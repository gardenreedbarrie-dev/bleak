Bleakhold Royale sound effects
==============================

Every sound in this folder is synthesised for Bleakhold by Tools/Announcer/make_royale_sfx.py from sine partials,
filtered noise and envelopes (fixed seeds, so the files are reproducible). No recording, sample or sound library is
used, and nothing is copied from any game.

  tick.wav               countdown 15..4: a soft forged tick over a low thump
  tick3/tick2/tick1.wav  the last three seconds: hammer on anvil, rising in pitch
  kill.wav               the player eliminates someone: a metallic clang and a thump
  wind.wav               the freefall's air rush (7.5 s: it starts 0.5 s after the DROP! gong and fades as the
                         parachute opens after the 8 s freefall)
  canopy.wav             the parachute opening: a fabric snap
  land.wav               the landing: a soft thud
  *_quiet.wav            the same 9 dB quieter (3.3.5a's PlaySoundFile has no volume argument)

Format: 22.05 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV, the loud part near -18 dBFS RMS, no peak over -1 dBFS.
To regenerate: python3 -I Tools/Announcer/make_royale_sfx.py (--check verifies the committed files).
