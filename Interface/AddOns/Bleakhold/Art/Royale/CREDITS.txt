Bleakhold Royale art
====================

Every texture in this folder is original art drawn for Bleakhold by its own generators, from shapes, height fields,
noise and the terrain of the Royale map; nothing is copied from any game or art library:

  Tools/BlpConvert/make_royale_hud_art.py  plates, buttons, the banner, numerals, DROP!, titles, the ready check's
                                           ring, the drop countdown's pull numerals, notches, gems, icons, markers, the
                                           parachute, the altimeter, speed lines, the storm edge (and
                                           Interface/AddOns/Bleakhold/RoyaleArtData.lua)
  Tools/BlpConvert/make_royale_art.py      the island map (IslandMap, MapThumb) from
                                           Tools/MapForge/specs/royale_v2.json, the storm rings and holes, the dashed
                                           next-circle rings

The numerals (NumeralsL/M/S), DROP! (DropWord), the drop countdown's pull numerals and DROP! (Pull) and the titles
(Titles) are set in Cinzel Bold, Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors (https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel),
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (LICENSE-OFL-Cinzel.txt next to this file). The font itself is
used only when the art is drawn (Tools/BlpConvert/fonts/Cinzel-Bold.ttf, from the @fontsource/cinzel package of Google
Fonts' ofl/cinzel, weight 700, Latin subset); its digits are aligned optically to one cap band by the generator. The art
is not the font and is not sold by itself.

The addon's live labels use Marcellus SC (Interface/AddOns/Bleakhold/Fonts, with its OFL.txt), Copyright (c) 2012
Brian J. Bonislawsky DBA Astigmatic (AOETI), with Reserved Font Names "Marcellus", SIL Open Font License 1.1.

To redraw: python3 -I Tools/BlpConvert/make_royale_hud_art.py [--preview DIR], then
python3 -I Tools/BlpConvert/make_royale_art.py [--cache FILE] (two minutes each; --check DIR verifies the committed
files against the generators with the same Pillow, FreeType and numpy builds).
